# ADR D07: Freeze production deploys on Fridays

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2024-09-05
- Owner: Marcus Chen (SRE)
- Systems: infra

## Context

- A rollback requires a manual database restore.

## Decision

No production deploy from Friday 12:00 to Monday 09:00.

## Consequences

Teams follow this until it is explicitly revisited. Exceptions need the owner's approval.
