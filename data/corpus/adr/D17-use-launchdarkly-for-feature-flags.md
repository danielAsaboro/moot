# ADR D17: Use LaunchDarkly for feature flags

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2025-06-10
- Owner: Marcus Chen (SRE)
- Systems: web-app

## Context

- LaunchDarkly costs under $500 a month at our seat count.

## Decision

Feature flags are managed in LaunchDarkly rather than a home-grown table.

## Consequences

Teams follow this until it is explicitly revisited. Exceptions need the owner's approval.
