# ADR D08: Keep the monolith; no new services

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2025-05-20
- Owner: Tomi Adeyemi (CTO)
- Systems: infra

## Context

- The engineering team is under 15 people.

## Decision

New features are built inside the monolith; service extraction proposals are declined.

## Consequences

Teams follow this until it is explicitly revisited. Exceptions need the owner's approval.
