# ADR D28: Deploy to a single region in Virginia

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2024-06-10
- Owner: Tomi Adeyemi (CTO)
- Systems: infra

## Context

We have no EU customers yet and one region is simpler to run.

_No premise was recorded for this decision._

## Decision

One AWS region, us-east-1, serves every customer.

## Consequences

Everything runs in us-east-1.
